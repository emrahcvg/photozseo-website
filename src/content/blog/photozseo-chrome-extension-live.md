**photoZseo for Chrome** is now on the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/photozseo/hmmeomfepgjaplheiolbibfkdbooioce). It brings the part of photoZseo that sellers do at a desk — collecting product photos and getting them ready for each marketplace — into your browser.

## What it does

- **Reads the product page you are viewing.** Open the side panel and it picks up the product name, brand, category, price, colour variants and every product image.
- **Resizes for marketplaces.** 82 resize and compression profiles with the right pixel size, ratio and file-size limit for each platform and region.
- **SEO file names and alt text.** Short, keyword-first file names built from the product name, brand, colour and category, plus alt text in your language.
- **Exports what you upload.** A folder-organised ZIP, plus CSV or Excel in 50 platform formats, including Shopify, Trendyol and Hepsiburada spreadsheets.
- **Groups colour variants** so Shopify options and marketplace variant group codes are filled for you.
- **Prices and margin.** Enter cost and sale price, apply a bulk markup with .90 or .99 rounding and see profit and margin per product.

## Privacy

There is no account and no photoZseo server. Your product library lives in your browser (IndexedDB). The extension reads a page only when you open the panel, start a scan or press the optional "+" button on an image. Access to all sites is optional and you can revoke it at any time. There is no analytics, tracking or advertising. Read the [extension privacy policy](/privacy/#chrome-extension).

## Pricing

| Plan | Price | Includes |

Prices are in US dollars. Pro is a subscription handled by Dodo Payments; cancel at any time and Pro stays active until the end of the paid period.

## Extension or app?

The two products complement each other. Use the **Chrome extension** at your computer to collect photos from product pages and export upload files. Use the **[iPhone and iPad app](/blog/photozseo-now-on-app-store)** to shoot new product photos with the template camera and remove backgrounds on the device. Background removal is not part of the extension.

## Requirements

Google Chrome 116 or later on Windows, macOS, Linux or ChromeOS (the extension uses the Chrome side panel, so it does not run on mobile). The interface is available in 15 languages.

## Get started

[Add photoZseo to Chrome](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/photozseo/hmmeomfepgjaplheiolbibfkdbooioce), open any product page and click the photoZseo icon. Details, screenshots and the full FAQ are on the [Chrome extension page](/chrome-extension).

Please only process images you have the right to use. Marketplace names describe compatibility only; photoZseo is not affiliated with or endorsed by any marketplace.
