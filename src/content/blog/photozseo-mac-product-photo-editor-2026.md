For most indie sellers, the bottleneck isn't shooting product photos — it's editing them. The iPhone captures studio-quality images all day, but moving them to Amazon, Shopify, Etsy and Trendyol still ends up on a desktop. Until now, that meant Photoshop, Lightroom, or a stack of paid web tools.

photoZseo's Mac Catalyst build closes that gap. The same app that you've been using on iPhone now runs natively on macOS 14+, with the keyboard shortcuts, drag-drop and inspector layout Mac users expect.

## What's actually native on Mac

A Catalyst app done right doesn't just open in a window. photoZseo on Mac picks up macOS conventions:

- **Keyboard shortcuts:** `⌘N` for a new project, `⌘E` to export, `⌘F` to search, `⌘A` to select every photo in the grid.
- **Finder integration:** Drag one photo, a multi-select or an entire folder onto the project window. photoZseo imports, dedupes by perceptual hash, runs OCR and EXIF in parallel.
- **Right-click context menus:** Rename, duplicate, push to a marketplace, copy SEO filename — all from the same menu Mac users already know.
- **3-pane inspector:** Sidebar with projects and contacts on the left, photo grid in the middle, detail panel with EXIF, SEO and pricing on the right. Resize panes the way you would in Finder or Mail.
- **Native window behavior:** Full-screen mode, Mission Control, multiple windows, copy-paste of SEO fields into any Mac app.

## Why a Mac version matters for sellers

The honest answer is throughput. Tapping through 200 photos on iPhone works for a small Etsy shop. For an Amazon FBA seller listing a new collection on Monday, batch is everything. On Mac, you can:

1. **Drop an entire shoot folder onto photoZseo** — 300 photos in, organized by date.
2. **Hit ⌘A then export** — every photo gets resized to the 46 marketplace presets at once, with WebP, JPEG and PNG written in parallel.
3. **Push selected products to Shopify** with one click — the [platform presets](/features/platform-presets) feature handles the per-platform spec.

What took an afternoon on a single device now takes the time to brew coffee.

## iCloud Drive keeps everything in sync

Move from iPhone in the studio to Mac at the desk without losing a thing. photoZseo syncs through your own iCloud Drive — projects, photos, contacts, quotes, even Recently Deleted items. There's no third-party account, no Firebase, no analytics layer. Last-write-wins reconciliation handles conflicts; deletes propagate across devices with a 30-day safety net so a wrong tap on iPhone doesn't permanently lose a photo on Mac.

For sellers who actually live in two places — the studio and the desk — that consistency is the difference between a tool you trust and a tool you don't.
