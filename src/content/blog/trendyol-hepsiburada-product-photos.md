Trendyol and Hepsiburada are Turkey's largest online marketplaces, and both review product images before a listing goes live. Unlike Amazon or eBay, they publish their detailed image rules **inside the seller panel** rather than on a public help page — so treat the panel as the source of truth and use the tips below as a starting point.

## Safe defaults

- **Main image on a white background**, product centred and filling most of the frame.
- **Square (1:1)** for most categories; **3:4 portrait** is common in fashion and shoes.
- **JPEG, sRGB**, sharp and well lit; no blur or heavy filters.
- **No watermarks, logos, borders or promotional text** ("indirim", "kargo bedava") on the product photo.
- **Several angles:** front, back, side, detail and label photos reduce questions and returns.

## Turkish SEO details

- **Titles** follow the pattern *Brand + product + key attribute + size/colour*.
- **File names in Turkish, transliterated:** `kirmizi-deri-cuzdan-on.jpg` rather than `IMG_0421.jpg` ([how to name product photos](/blog/how-to-name-product-photos-for-seo)).
- **Alt text and descriptions in Turkish**, written for the shopper's search terms.
