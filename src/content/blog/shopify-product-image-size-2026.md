Shopify is flexible about image sizes, which is exactly why stores end up with mismatched product grids. A simple rule avoids most problems.

## The recommended size

Shopify recommends **2048 × 2048 px** for square product photos ([Shopify Help: Uploading images](https://help.shopify.com/manual/using-themes/images)). That size:

- is large enough for the zoom feature in most themes,
- matches the square frame most themes use in collection grids,
- leaves room for Shopify to create smaller versions for phones.

## Keep one aspect ratio

Most Shopify themes crop or pad product images to the aspect ratio of the first image. Mixing portrait, landscape and square photos produces uneven rows. Pick one ratio — **1:1 square** is the safest — and use it for every product.

## File size and speed

Shopify's CDN resizes images for each device, but large originals still slow down uploads and the admin. Practical targets:

- **JPEG, sRGB, quality 80–85%.**
- **200–600 KB per image** after compression.
- **Descriptive file names** (`walnut-cutting-board-large.jpg`) and **alt text** for every image — both help Google Images.
