**To optimize a product listing, fix the title first, then the images, then the attributes, and write the description last.** The title and main image decide whether a buyer clicks; the attributes decide whether the platform can match you to a search or filter. This checklist covers each part in the order you should work, with the platform limits I could verify from official sources.

*Last verified: November 2026. Sources: Google Merchant Center Help ("Title [title]" and "Image [image_link]"), eBay Developers Program (Trading API user guide, "Listing title"), Shopify Help Center ("Product media types"), U.S. Copyright Office FAQ. Platform rules change; check your channel's current guidelines before bulk uploading.*

## What goes in a product title?

Use a repeatable formula so every listing in the catalogue is consistent:

**Brand + product type + key attribute (model, material, size) + colour or variant**

Example: `Nordhaus Walnut Cutting Board, Large 18 in, Juice Groove`.

Rules that hold on almost every channel:

- Put the most important words at the start. Google notes that users usually see only the first 70 or so characters, depending on screen size.
- Leave out price, sale dates, shipping details and your store name. Google's Merchant Center guidance says to submit those through their own attributes.
- Avoid all caps, symbols and promotional wording.
- Match what is actually in the box. Variants (colour, size, pack count) belong in the title only when they differentiate the item.

## What do the images need?

- A clean main image of the product itself, not a logo or placeholder.
- Extra angles: front, back, side, detail, scale, and a packaging or in-use shot where the platform allows it.
- No watermarks, promotional overlays, barcodes, QR codes or added store logos. Google's image guidance lists these as prohibited for Merchant Center.
- Consistent background, crop and lighting across the catalogue.
- A file type that your platform accepts, with the extension matching the real format.

Resize for each channel rather than uploading originals. See [marketplace image sizes](/marketplace-image-sizes), and for format choices read [WebP vs JPEG vs PNG for product photos](/blog/webp-jpeg-png-product-photos-2026).

## How do I fill in attributes?

Attributes are the structured fields: brand, category, material, colour, size, GTIN or model number, condition. They power filters and matching, and incomplete ones are a common reason a listing is suppressed or ranks poorly.

1. Use the platform's own category and taxonomy, not a custom label.
2. Fill every field you can answer truthfully, including optional ones.
3. Use consistent values (one spelling of a colour, one unit system).
4. Keep identifiers (GTIN, SKU, model number) accurate.

## How should I write the description?

Lead with what the product is and who it is for in the first two sentences. Then list specifications in a short bullet list or table, then care, compatibility and what is included. Use plain language, write original copy rather than pasting manufacturer or supplier text, and avoid claims you cannot support.

## What about alt text and file names?

- **Alt text:** one sentence describing what is visible, specific to that image. "Walnut cutting board, top view, juice groove along the edge" is better than "cutting board". Do not repeat the same phrase across all photos. More examples are in [product image alt text examples](/blog/product-image-alt-text-examples).
- **File names:** lowercase, hyphens between words, brand or product first, then the view or variant. See [how to name product photos for SEO](/blog/how-to-name-product-photos-for-seo).

## The one-page listing checklist

- [ ] Title follows the formula, important words first, no promo text
- [ ] Main image is the product alone, compliant, correct size
- [ ] 4 or more additional images where allowed (angles, detail, scale)
- [ ] No watermarks, logos or overlays that the channel forbids
- [ ] Every attribute filled with consistent values
- [ ] Identifiers (GTIN, SKU, model) verified
- [ ] Description is original, specific and scannable
- [ ] Unique alt text per image
- [ ] Descriptive file names
- [ ] You have the right to use every image
