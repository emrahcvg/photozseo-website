Alt text does two jobs: it tells screen-reader users what a photo shows, and it tells search engines what the image is about. Google's [image SEO best practices](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/google-images) describe alt text as the most important metadata for an image — and warn against stuffing it with keywords.

## The formula

**Product type + key attribute(s) + view or context**

Keep it to one short phrase. Don't start with "image of" or "photo of" — screen readers already announce it as an image ([Google's guide to writing alt text](https://developers.google.com/tech-writing/accessibility/self-study/write-alt-text)).

## Examples

| Product | Bad alt text | Good alt text |
|---|---|---|
| T-shirt | `tshirt` | Men's navy cotton crew-neck T-shirt, front view |
| T-shirt | `best cheap tshirt mens tshirt cotton tshirt` | Close-up of navy cotton T-shirt fabric and stitching |
| Sneakers | `shoes` | White leather low-top sneakers, side view |
| Sneakers | `IMG_2231` | White sneakers worn with cuffed jeans on a city street |
| Cutting board | `product` | Large walnut cutting board with juice groove, top view |
| Cutting board | `cutting board cutting board wood` | Walnut cutting board with sliced bread on a kitchen counter |
| Mug | `mug1` | 350 ml white ceramic mug with matte finish |
| Headphones | `headphones` | Black over-ear wireless headphones folded flat |
| Headphones | `buy now 50% off` | Headphones' USB-C port and control buttons, close-up |
| Necklace | `jewelry` | Gold-plated chain necklace with small pearl pendant |
| Lamp | `lamp photo` | Brass desk lamp with adjustable arm, lit, on a wooden desk |
| Backpack | `bag` | Grey 20-litre roll-top backpack showing laptop sleeve |

## Rules of thumb

- **Describe the photo, not the listing.** Price, discounts and "free shipping" don't belong in alt text.
- **Make each image unique** — front, side, detail and lifestyle shots show different things.
- **Use the shopper's words:** "crew-neck", "roll-top", "matte".
- **Write it in the page's language** for each localized store.
