**photoZseo: SEO Product Photo** is now available on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6762639768) for iPhone and iPad.

It is built for one job that every online seller repeats every day: taking a product photo and preparing it for each marketplace — the right size, the right background, a searchable file name, and listing text that marketplaces and search engines can read.

## What photoZseo does

- **Template camera.** A guided camera walks you through the 7 angles marketplaces ask for: front, back, side, detail, top, label and lifestyle.
- **46 marketplaces from one photo.** Each photo is cropped, resized, compressed and renamed for every marketplace you sell on — Amazon, eBay, Etsy, Shopify, Walmart, Trendyol, Hepsiburada, AliExpress, Temu, SHEIN, Mercado Libre, Allegro and more, plus Instagram, TikTok Shop, Pinterest and WhatsApp Catalog.
- **Background removal on the device.** Transparent PNG, pure white or a custom color — for one photo or a whole batch.
- **13 SEO fields in 15 languages.** Title, slug, file name, category, meta description, description, alt text, tags, keywords and barcode.
- **Listing files.** CSV, XLSX and JSON in each marketplace's column layout, per product or combined.
- **For B2B and Shopify sellers.** Branded PDF quotes with VAT, IBAN, Incoterms and HS codes, and direct product push to Shopify.

Photo processing and background removal run on your iPhone or iPad, and the app works offline. On iOS 26 with Apple Intelligence, listing text is written by Apple's on-device model.

## Requirements

- iPhone with iOS 17.0 or later, or iPad with iPadOS 17.0 or later
- Available in 15 languages: English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Polish, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Arabic, Hindi, Persian and Urdu
- Not yet available for Android or Mac. On a computer, use the separate [photoZseo Chrome extension](/chrome-extension)
