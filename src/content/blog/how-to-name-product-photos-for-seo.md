Most product photos are uploaded as `IMG_0421.jpg`. That wastes a free signal search engines use to understand the image.

## What Google says

Google's [image SEO best practices](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/google-images) recommend **short but descriptive file names**: `my-new-black-kitten.jpg` is better than `IMG00023.JPG`. Google also recommends **translating file names** for localized pages and, for large sites, **automating** the naming.

## A naming pattern for products

Use this order, in lowercase, with hyphens between words:

`brand-product-key-attribute-view.jpg`

| Bad | Good |
|---|---|
| `IMG_0421.jpg` | `acme-walnut-cutting-board-large-front.jpg` |
| `photo1.png` | `acme-walnut-cutting-board-large-side.jpg` |
| `DSC08812.JPG` | `nova-ceramic-mug-350ml-white-lifestyle.jpg` |

Tips:

- **Keep it short** — 3 to 6 meaningful words.
- **Add the view** (front, back, side, detail, label, lifestyle) so each image in a listing has a unique name.
- **Don't stuff keywords.** One clear description beats a list of synonyms.
- **Avoid special characters** and spaces; non-Latin letters are best transliterated for URLs.
- **Translate for each market**, e.g. `kirmizi-deri-cuzdan-on.jpg` for a Turkish store.

## File name, alt text and title work together

The file name is one of three text signals on a product image. The other two are [alt text](/blog/product-image-alt-text-examples) and the text around the image on the page. Keep all three consistent.
