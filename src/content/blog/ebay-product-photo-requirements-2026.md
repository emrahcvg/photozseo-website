eBay is less strict about backgrounds than Amazon, but its picture policy has a hard minimum size and a few rules that block or downrank listings. This is what eBay's own help pages say.

## eBay image size

- **Minimum:** at least one photo of **500 pixels on the longest side** ([eBay picture policy](https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/listing-policies/picture-policy?id=4370)).
- **Recommended:** about **1600 × 1600 px** ([Adding pictures to your listings](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/listings/adding-pictures-listings?id=4148)).
- **Regional guidance:** eBay UK suggests 800–1600 px on the longest side; eBay Canada recommends 1600 px so photos look good on mobile.

A square photo works best: eBay's search results and gallery display images in a square frame.

## What eBay does not allow

- **Borders and added text** on the photo.
- **Watermarks**, including your shop name.
- **Stock photos for used or defective items** — buyers need to see the real item and its condition.

## Checklist before you list

1. Export a square JPEG of about 1600 × 1600 px.
2. Use a plain, uncluttered background (white or light grey works well in search results).
3. Remove any watermark, frame or promotional text.
4. Show flaws clearly on used items — it reduces returns.
5. Give the file a descriptive name, such as `vintage-leather-camera-bag-front.jpg`, before uploading.
