If you advertise or list products on Google Shopping, check your image sizes now. Google is raising the minimum.

## What is changing

According to Google's [image_link specification](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6324350?hl=en) and its [2026 product data specification update](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/16989427?hl=en):

- From **31 January 2027**, images submitted in `image_link` and `additional_image_link` must be at least **500 × 500 pixels**.
- The requirement applies to **all product categories**.
- **Additional images** must meet the same size, file and URL requirements as the main image.

Google also recommends larger images — around **1500 × 1500 px or more** — so products look sharp across Shopping formats.

## Who is affected

Mostly catalogs with older or thumbnail-sized images: imports from legacy systems, supplier feeds, or images resized down for page speed. If your feed points at a 300 × 300 thumbnail, it will fail the new rule.

## How to prepare

1. **Audit your feed.** Export the image URLs and check the pixel size of each one.
2. **Point the feed at full-size images**, not thumbnails.
3. **Re-export small images** from the originals at 1500 × 1500 px or larger.
4. **Use the real product** as the main image — no logos, placeholders or "image coming soon" graphics.
5. **Keep the rest of the image rules:** no promotional text, watermarks or borders on the main image.
