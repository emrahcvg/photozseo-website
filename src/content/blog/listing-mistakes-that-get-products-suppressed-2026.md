A product listing is usually suppressed or rejected for one of four reasons: a photo that breaks the channel's image rules (non-white main background, watermark, border or added text), an undersized image, missing or mismatched attributes, or a title and category the platform's policy does not allow. The reason is almost always stated in your seller dashboard or email, and fixing that single item and resubmitting is normally enough.

*Last verified: November 2026. Rules change; where a number is not listed below, we could not confirm it on an official page, so check the platform's current guidelines.*

## What do the official pages say?

We checked the marketplaces' own help and policy pages. Here is what they confirm.

| Marketplace | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| eBay | At least one photo of 500 px on the longest side; recommended about 1600 x 1600; up to 24 photos; max 12 MB per photo | eBay Picture policy; eBay help "Adding pictures to your listings"; eBay Seller Center "Take great photos" |
| eBay | Prohibited: photos that don't accurately represent the item, placeholder images used to convey messages, stock photos for used/damaged/defective items, added borders, added text/artwork/marketing material, watermarks of any type including ownership attributions | eBay Picture policy |
| Etsy | Recommended listing photos at least 2000 px wide and tall; first photo at least 635 px to avoid ranking lower; files over 1 MB may not finish uploading on slow connections; sRGB; no animated GIF or transparent PNG | Etsy Help, "Requirements and Best Practices for Images in Your Etsy Shop" |
| Etsy | At least one unedited photo of the actual item against a real background | Etsy Help, same article |
| Amazon | When a main image is uploaded, Amazon checks for non-white backgrounds and for text, logos, graphics or watermarks; images that don't meet requirements may lead to suppression | Amazon Seller Central announcement on main image checks |

Amazon's exact pixel and frame-fill requirements vary by category and we could not verify a current number from an official page, so none is quoted here. Open the image guidelines for your category in Seller Central.

## Why do Amazon listings get suppressed?

Amazon states that images not meeting its requirements may lead to suppression and that it automatically checks main images for non-white backgrounds and text, logos, graphics or watermarks. Beyond images, sellers discussing suppression in the Seller Forums commonly list these causes (this is community advice, not an Amazon policy page, so use it as a checklist rather than a rule):

- Missing required attributes for the category (size, color, material)
- Titles or bullets with restricted claims, excess symbols or prohibited terms
- A product placed in the wrong category or product type
- Missing compliance documents or safety information in regulated categories

To see the actual reason, use the suppressed-listings view in Seller Central (Manage Inventory, then fix suppressed listings) rather than guessing. Sellers also report an inventory report named "Listing Quality and Suppressed Listing Report" that shows the failing field. Some image suppressions may not appear in Account Health, so check the listing-level view as well.

## Why does eBay block listing photos?

eBay's Picture policy is the clearest of the three. Its prohibited list covers photos with added borders, added text, artwork or marketing material, and watermarks "of any type, including those used for ownership attributions." That means a small corner logo is still a violation. It also prohibits stock photos for used, damaged or defective items and photos that don't accurately represent the item.

If you reuse one export across channels, the usual culprit is a branded or bordered image made for another platform. Re-export a clean copy from your master photo.

## Why does Etsy remove listings?

On Etsy, removals most often trace back to what is being sold rather than how it is photographed. Etsy's help article on reasons a listing may be removed lists items that cannot be sold as craft supplies, uncustomized versions of mass-made modern items, items assembled only from a manufacturer's pieces and instructions, and reselling a pre-existing design. Etsy emails the reason; removed listings appear under Policy violations in Shop Manager.

Photo-wise, Etsy requires at least one unedited photo of the actual item against a real background, and recommends 2000 px photos. A first photo under 635 px is not a removal trigger but can rank lower.

## Which mistakes cause rejections, by category?

| Mistake | Typical effect | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Watermark, logo or promo text on the photo | eBay prohibits; Amazon main-image check flags | Export a clean copy without overlays |
| Added border or frame | eBay prohibits | Remove the border; crop to the product |
| Non-white main background (Amazon) | Auto-flagged, can suppress | Re-shoot or remove the background and place on pure white |
| Photo below minimum size | Upload fails or listing rejected | Export at or above the channel's minimum (eBay 500 px; Etsy 635 px for the first photo) |
| Stock or placeholder photo | eBay prohibits for used/damaged items | Photograph the actual item |
| Photo doesn't match the item or variant | Misrepresentation | Show only the variant sold |
| Missing required attributes | Suppression | Complete required fields for the category |
| Heavy edits, no real-item photo (Etsy) | Fails authenticity guidance | Add one unedited photo on a real background |
| Wrong category | Required attributes unmatched | Re-select the category |

## Fix-it checklist before you resubmit

1. Read the exact error or email text; do not guess.
2. Open the image on a plain screen and look for any text, logo, watermark or border, including faint corner marks.
3. Confirm dimensions and file size against the channel's current page.
4. Make sure title, photo and attributes describe the same item, color and quantity.
5. Confirm the category and required fields.
6. Re-upload from a clean export of your master photo, not a previously edited copy.
