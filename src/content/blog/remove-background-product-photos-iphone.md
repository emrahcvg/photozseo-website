A clean background is the single biggest upgrade for most product photos. You can do it on your iPhone without a desktop editor.

## 1. The built-in Photos cut-out (free)

Apple's Photos app can separate a subject from its background ([Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/lift-a-subject-from-the-photo-background-iphfe4809658/ios)):

1. Open the photo in full screen.
2. **Touch and hold the product** until an outline appears.
3. Tap **Copy** or **Share**.

It works in Photos, Safari, Quick Look and other apps, and requires iOS 16 or later on iPhone XS, XR and newer ([Apple: Create and share photo cutouts](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102460)).

**Limitations for sellers:** it handles one photo at a time, the result is a transparent cut-out (not a marketplace-ready white image), and you still need to resize and rename it.

## 2. Put the product on pure white

Amazon and Walmart require the main image on **pure white, RGB 255, 255, 255** — not off-white and not transparent. After cutting out the product:

1. Place it on a white canvas.
2. Centre it and let it fill about 85% of the frame (Amazon's guidance).
3. Export a **JPEG** at the marketplace's size (e.g. 2000 × 2000 px).

Keep **transparent PNGs** for your own website, ads and compositions.

## Tips for better cut-outs

- Shoot against a **plain, contrasting background** — a sheet of paper or a wall is enough.
- Use **soft, even light**; hard shadows confuse edge detection.
- Keep the **whole product in frame** with a little margin.
- Check **fine details** (straps, cables, hair, glass) before publishing.
