Selling on several marketplaces usually means editing the same photo again and again. A simple workflow fixes that: **one master photo, many exports.**

## Step 1: Shoot one good master

- **At least 2000 px on the shortest side.** Every marketplace version is then a downscale, never an upscale.
- **Square framing with margin**, so you can crop to 1:1 or 3:4 later.
- **Plain background and even light** — it makes the white main image easy.

## Step 2: Make two kinds of images

1. **Main image:** product only, pure white background (RGB 255, 255, 255), product filling most of the frame. Required by Amazon and Walmart; works everywhere.
2. **Secondary images:** other angles, details, scale and lifestyle shots.

## Step 3: Know the targets

| Marketplace | Size to export |
|---|---|
| Amazon | 2000 × 2000 px, white background |
| eBay | ≈ 1600 × 1600 px (min. 500 px) |
| Etsy | 2000 px or larger |
| Shopify | 2048 × 2048 px |
| Trendyol / Hepsiburada | Check the seller panel; square or 3:4 |

Official sources for each are in our [marketplace image size guide](/marketplace-image-sizes).

## Step 4: Export each version properly

For every marketplace:

- **Resize** to the target size and aspect ratio.
- **Convert** to JPEG (sRGB); use PNG only where transparency is needed.
- **Compress** to roughly 200–600 KB.
- **Rename** descriptively: `acme-walnut-board-large-front.jpg` ([how to name product photos](/blog/how-to-name-product-photos-for-seo)).
- **Strip GPS** and other private metadata.
